# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in our community and its projects will be treated with respect and dignity.

We are committed to creating a safe and supportive environment for everyone, regardless of:
- Gender, gender identity or expression
- Sexual orientation
- Disability
- Physical appearance
- Body size
- Race or ethnicity
- Age
- Religion or lack thereof
- Education level
- Socioeconomic status
- National origin
- Experience level
- Marital status
- Political affiliation

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Being patient with those asking questions
- Assuming good intentions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment of any kind
- Discrimination of any kind
- Intimidation or threats
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory remarks
- Sexual content or language
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which is inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. This includes:

- Addressing inappropriate behavior
- Removing offensive comments
- Banning users for repeated violations

Community leaders will:
- Investigate reports promptly
- Maintain confidentiality
- Communicate decisions fairly
- Take appropriate action

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:
- **Email**: a1009us@gmail.com
- **GitHub Issues**: Use "Report" template
- **Direct Message**: Contact maintainers directly

Include:
- Description of what happened
- Who was involved
- When it occurred
- Any relevant context

All reports will be taken seriously and investigated promptly.

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- Warning
- Temporary ban from community
- Permanent removal from community

The severity of consequences will depend on the nature and severity of the violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All community spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, PRs)
- Official community events
- Community communications
- Representation in the community
